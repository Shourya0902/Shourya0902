### 📂 Featured Projects

**🔹 [LLM-Statistics-RAG-Evaluation](https://github.com/Shourya0902/LLM-Statistics-RAG-Evaluation)**
*Can LLMs learn statistics through RAG, or does external context cause interference?*
- **Tech:** Python, ChromaDB, Cross-Encoders, LLM-as-a-Judge pipeline
- **Models tested:** LLaMA 3.2, DeepSeek-R1, Qwen2-Math
- **Key finding:** Math-specialized models suffer performance drops with RAG due to cognitive conflict — RAG isn't always better

**🔹 [Boston Housing Price Prediction](https://github.com/Shourya0902/boston_housing_price_prediction)**
*End-to-end ML app predicting housing prices, served via FastAPI + Streamlit, containerised with Docker, deploying to AWS*
- **Tech:** Python, scikit-learn, FastAPI, Streamlit, Docker, AWS
- **Results:** R² = 0.711 | RMSE = 4.64 | MAE = 3.16
- **Live demo:** Coming soon (AWS deployment in progress)

**🔹 [Hotel Reviews Topic Modeling](https://github.com/Shourya0902/hotel-reviews-topic-modeling)**
*NLP analysis of 10,000+ hotel reviews across 15 languages using LDA topic modeling*
- **Tech:** R, tm, topicmodels, ldatuning, GloVe
- **Key finding:** Customers evaluate hotels holistically — all 10 factors carry near-equal weight (~10%)

**🔹 [PCE Time Series Forecasting](https://github.com/Shourya0902/PCE-Time-Series-Forecasting)**
*Forecasting $21.4 trillion in US consumer spending across 65+ years of economic data*
- **Tech:** R, ARIMA, Holt-Winters, cross-validation
- **Results:** 73% RMSE improvement over baseline | Predicted 4.5% annual growth for 2025

**🔹 [Zoho CRM ETL Pipeline](https://github.com/Shourya0902/zoho-etl-pipeline)**
